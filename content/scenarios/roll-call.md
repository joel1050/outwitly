# Roll Call

- **id:** `roll-call`
- **Family:** voting
- **Source:** Gemini 4 Neon (xhigh) proposal, 2026-11-25. Original: `docs/proposals/gemini-4-neon.md`.
- **Status:** see `content/catalog.json`

The town picks one project by a public show of hands. Each voter sees the running count.

- **Goal** — Get the Park built. Second best: the Pool.
- **Setup** — Three projects stand left to right: Mall, Pool, Park. You vote first, then Greedy, Loner, Copycat and Show-off, and each sees the votes so far. The most votes wins; the leftmost wins a tie.
- **Your choice** — Vote Mall, Pool or Park.
- **Characters and rules**
  - Greedy votes for the Pool.
  - Loner votes for the project with the fewest votes, leftmost on a tie.
  - Copycat votes the same as Loner.
  - Show-off votes for the project with the most votes, rightmost on a tie.
- **How it resolves** — Count your vote, then count each character's vote in the order listed in the round. The winning project determines your value. Park scores 100, Pool scores 50 and Mall scores 0.
- **Round results** — Vote Mall and Park wins 3–1–1. Vote Pool and Pool wins 3–2 over Mall. Vote Park and Mall wins 3–1–1.
- **Idea** — Vote Mall to make Park the only project with zero votes when Loner moves. Loner and Copycat give Park the lead, and Show-off adds the third vote.
- **Can vary** — Change the three or four projects, their left-to-right order, your preference values, Greedy's favourite, the voter order and Copycat's target. Place the target before Copycat.
- **Scene** — Each voter raises a hand in turn, a badge drops onto the corresponding project banner, and confetti appears over the winner.
