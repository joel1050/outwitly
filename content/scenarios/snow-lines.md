# Snow Lines

- **id:** `snow-lines`
- **Family:** conflict
- **Source:** GPT-6.2 Sol (high) proposal, 2026-11-25. Original: `docs/proposals/gpt-6.2-sol.md`.
- **Status:** see `content/catalog.json`

- **Story**  
  Two snowball teams line up across three lanes to capture flags.
- **Goal**  
  Win flags worth the most points. Higher is better.
- **Setup**  
  Flags pay 3, 10, 6 left to right. Pick your lineup using strengths 5, 3, 1. Cautious sees it and places strengths 6, 4, 2. Higher strength wins each flag. Cautious wins ties. Fill gaps left to right.
- **Your choice**  
  Set your left-to-right lineup to 1/5/3, 1/3/5, or 5/3/1.
- **Characters and rules**
  - Cautious — Matches its strongest to yours, then fills gaps strongest first.
- **How it resolves**  
  Each side has three distinct thrower strengths. Put Cautious’s strongest thrower opposite your strongest, then put its second strongest in the leftmost open lane and its weakest in the last lane. Compare strengths in each lane, awarding the flag to Cautious on equality. Your value is the total printed points of your flags.
- **Trap**  
  Putting your strongest on the 10-point flag draws an even stronger defender there.
- **Idea**  
  Draw the strongest defender away.
- **Can vary**  
  Use three distinct strengths per team from 1–9, distinct flag values from 1–12, and two to four distinct permutations of your lineup. Include lineups that put your strongest on the most valuable flag. Change whether your strongest can beat theirs and which other head-to-head wins are possible. If your strongest beats theirs, tag the variant where it wins the main flag “Win the direct matchup.”
- **Scene**  
  Slide the teams into the three lanes. Throw one volley, then raise the winning flags.
- **Difficulty range**  
  Easy when your strongest loses and only one other thrower can score. Medium when two lanes can pay and you must compare their combined value.
- **Rating**  
  Fun 4/5 · Round variety 5/5. Placement draws a predictable defender, and the strength and point changes can reward a decoy or a straight win.
